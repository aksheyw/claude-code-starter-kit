# Agree the shape first

*Trigger: a deliverable going through rounds.*

Agree the words and the structure before anything gets formatted, and keep a written list of every ask.

**Why this exists.** Formatting locks in structure, so after layout every edit costs a rebuild and people defend sentences to avoid the rework. And past about three rounds, each version quietly reverses an earlier fix, because nothing holds the whole set.

**How to apply it.**

- The sequence: agree the headline and section order, one line each, then the body in plain text, then format. If I hand you slides before you have seen the sentences, send it back. When the look is the point, the visual leads.
- Build for skimming from draft one: every substantive bullet opens with a bold label, then the point. Tables for comparisons, a picture for anything structural.
- One to two readable pages per section, proof in an appendix. Cutting means removing content, not reformatting it.
- Past three asks, write them as a checkable list we can both see, and run the whole list before every version, not just the newest item.
- Answering someone else's brief: use their words and section names, lead with whatever they named first, and walk it line by line before either of us calls it done.

# Get it out of the chat

Start a new conversation for each new job, and put anything that matters in a file before you close the last one.

**Why this exists.** In chat, one endless thread is normal and mostly harmless. Here it is not. A conversation is a workspace, not a filing cabinet: once it closes the reasoning is gone and only the polished output survives, which is the part you could have rebuilt anyway. A session carrying three unrelated jobs also answers worse and costs more, because all of it gets read again on every turn.

**How to apply it.**

- New job, new session. When one job is done, start fresh rather than turning the same conversation to something else.
- Nothing from a conversation carries over. What carries is what is on disk, so anything that has to survive goes into a file before you close it.
- Substantial means a decision and its reason, a number you will quote later, research findings, verbatim customer feedback. Keep the raw as well as the summary, and save research with its source link and whether you opened that source, which catches the link nobody read.
- Two files hold most of it: `_hot.md` for where things stand right now, and `_log.md` for one short entry per session on what changed and what was not obvious. What you chose, what you rejected and why goes under "Decisions already made" in CLAUDE.md, so it is read at the start of every conversation. Once that section outgrows a page, move it to its own decisions file and point at it. If the project has none of these, ask for them, and put an instruction to read them in CLAUDE.md (rule 03) so they get read.
- The rejected options are the part that pays off later. Without them a decision reads as arbitrary preference and gets reversed by whoever forgot the reason, usually you.
- Type `/usage` to see how much of your allowance is left. It is shared with the Claude chat you already use, and shared across models, so switching to a smaller model does not give it back.

# Delegate the reading, keep the judgement

*Trigger: work you want run in parallel.*

Hand off work that is wide, repetitive or independent. Keep every call about what it means.

**Why this exists.** Your scarce resource is attention, not output, so spending it on mechanical reading is waste. But hand off the decision and you end up repeating a helper's conclusion as your own.

**How to apply it.**

- I can start helpers: separate Claude sessions that each do one job and report back. Ask in plain words: "start three helpers, one per folder, each listing the files that touch payments."
- Good reasons: the work can run in parallel, it is bulky and mechanical, or it needs eyes that have not already formed a view. The noise also stays out of our conversation.
- Bad reasons: reassurance, and work I could just finish myself. A helper double checking checked work returns a confident opinion, not evidence. One helper beats three when one is enough.
- A helper starts blank. Every request carries the context, the goal behind the task, what a finished answer looks like, and what it must not change or decide alone.
- Read what comes back as a claim. A helper that could not find something often produces a confident answer instead of admitting it looked and failed. Ask for the source beside the answer.
